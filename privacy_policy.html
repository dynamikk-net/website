  2. Github acts as a processor [Art. 4(8) UK GDPR] for network data (e.g. a user's IP address) relating to a user's browsing of Dynamikk Limited's website.
  3. Github's DPA and Privacy Statement apply.
  4. If a user sends an email to Dynamikk Limited or submits a form on Dynamikk Limited's website, Dynamikk Limited makes use of AWS Lambda, AWS SES, and AWS S3 when handling the email (respectively submission). The data centre eu-west-2 (London) is used.
  5. AWS acts as a processor [Art. 4(8) UK GDPR] for data sent to Dynamikk Limited over email or via Dynamikk Limited's website, including network data (e.g. a user's IP address).
  6. AWS's DPA and Data Privacy FAQ apply.
  7. We refer to GitHub's DPA and AWS's DPA for whether the data referred to in 2.b and 2.d may be transferred by GitHub and AWS, and the safeguards governing this.
  8. Dynamikk Limited may make use of Google Ads for marketing. No cookies or embedded code from Google are used.

  2. Dynamikk Limited can be contacted at contact@dynamikk.net for requests falling under 3.a or regarding this privacy policy.
  3. To file a complaint regarding Dynamikk Limited's compliance with UK GDPR, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) can be contacted.
